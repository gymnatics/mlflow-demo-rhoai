# ANZ New Zealand Lending Policy (Sample)

**Document ID:** POL-LEND-2025-001
**Version:** 3.2
**Classification:** Internal Use Only
**Last Reviewed:** January 2025

## 1. Personal Loans

### 1.1 Eligibility Criteria

- Applicant must be 18 years or older and a New Zealand resident or citizen.
- Minimum annual income of NZD 30,000 (gross).
- Employment tenure of at least 6 months with current employer, or 12 months of self-employment history.
- Satisfactory credit history with no defaults in the last 24 months.
- Debt-to-income ratio must not exceed 40%.

### 1.2 Loan Limits

- Minimum loan amount: NZD 2,000
- Maximum unsecured personal loan: NZD 50,000
- Maximum secured personal loan: NZD 100,000
- Standard term: 12 to 60 months

### 1.3 Interest Rate Framework

- Rates are determined by credit risk tier (A through D).
- Tier A (excellent credit): Base rate + 2.5%
- Tier B (good credit): Base rate + 4.0%
- Tier C (fair credit): Base rate + 6.5%
- Tier D (limited credit): Base rate + 9.0%
- All rates are subject to review and may change at the Bank's discretion.

## 2. Home Loans

### 2.1 Eligibility Criteria

- Applicant must be 18 years or older and a New Zealand resident, citizen, or hold a qualifying visa.
- Property must be located in New Zealand.
- Satisfactory credit history and stable income verification required.
- First-home buyers may qualify for reduced deposit requirements under the First Home Loan scheme.

### 2.2 Loan-to-Value Ratios (LVR)

- Owner-occupied: Maximum LVR of 80% (standard), up to 90% with lender's mortgage insurance.
- Investment property: Maximum LVR of 65%.
- First-home buyers: Up to 95% LVR under eligible government schemes.

### 2.3 Serviceability Assessment

- The Bank applies a serviceability buffer of 2.5% above the prevailing rate.
- All existing financial commitments are included in the assessment.
- Living expenses are assessed using the Household Expenditure Measure (HEM) or actual declared expenses, whichever is higher.

## 3. Responsible Lending Obligations

### 3.1 CCCFA Compliance

All lending decisions must comply with the Credit Contracts and Consumer Finance Act 2003 (CCCFA) and subsequent amendments, including:

- Conducting a suitability assessment before offering credit.
- Verifying the borrower's income and existing obligations.
- Ensuring the loan is not unsuitable for the borrower's requirements and objectives.
- Providing clear and transparent disclosure of fees, interest, and total cost of credit.

### 3.2 Hardship Provisions

- Borrowers experiencing financial hardship may apply for temporary repayment adjustments.
- Applications must be assessed within 5 working days.
- Options include interest-only periods, term extensions, or temporary payment reductions.

## 4. Approval Authority

| Loan Amount | Approval Level |
|-------------|---------------|
| Up to NZD 50,000 | Branch Lending Manager |
| NZD 50,001 - NZD 250,000 | Regional Credit Manager |
| NZD 250,001 - NZD 1,000,000 | Senior Credit Committee |
| Over NZD 1,000,000 | Executive Credit Committee |

## 5. Documentation Requirements

- Government-issued photo identification
- Proof of address (utility bill or bank statement, less than 3 months old)
- Income verification (3 months of payslips or 2 years of financial statements for self-employed)
- Asset and liability statement
- Property valuation (for secured lending)
